Find the diamond privacy policy

Updated October 12, 2026. This policy covers the upcoming update with advertising and optional Remove Ads purchases.

Find the diamond works without a game account. Your farm save stays on your device. Advertising and optional App Store purchases use Google and Apple services as described below.

Advertising

When advertising is enabled, the game uses Google AdMob to load and show ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices. It checks Google's consent status before requesting ads. The game requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier and does not ask for Apple's tracking permission. Ads are not requested when advertising is disabled; verified ad removal stops future ad requests.

Advertising data

Google may process your IP address and approximate location, app/device identifiers, ads viewed and interactions, and crash, performance and diagnostic information to provide and measure advertising and operate its services. Consent choices are also processed and saved. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. The game does not request access to contacts, device location services, photos, camera or microphone.

Purchases

Apple handles the optional permanent Remove Ads purchase. The game reads Apple's verified purchase history to activate removal, restore purchases and handle refunds or revocations. The game does not receive your payment card details or send purchase records to an advertising service. Apple's App Store privacy terms apply.

Saved on your device

Your world seed, excavation, machinery, cargo, in-game finances, statistics and preferences are stored in local app files. The seed, pile number and starting material quantity are also kept in this device's Keychain to preserve the hidden diamond's identity. Ad-break elapsed time is saved locally. Your farm save is not sent to Google or a developer server.

Local diagnostics

When developer performance recording is explicitly enabled, the game writes local frame timing, equipment counts and device thermal reports. These reports are not uploaded. Google's SDK diagnostics are separate and described above.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app using device Settings removes its local files; offloading keeps app data. iOS may retain the Keychain world identity after deletion. Backups follow your device settings. The developer has no server copy of your farm save to delete. Deletion does not revoke an App Store purchase or erase data already held by Google or Apple; their privacy policies explain retention and deletion.

Your choices

When Google requires it, Advertising privacy choices in Settings lets you review or change consent. Remove Ads and Restore purchases are available in Settings and before each ad. If an ad cannot load, play continues. Sound, haptics and reduced motion are optional. Apple's App Store, TestFlight, backups and device diagnostics follow Apple's privacy settings and policies.
