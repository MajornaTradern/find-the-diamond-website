Find the diamond privacy policy

Updated October 9, 2026.

Find the diamond works without an account. The game does not transmit your gameplay or personal information to the developer or other companies.

Data collection

The app has no advertising, tracking or analytics services, and no third-party SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Saved on your device

Your world seed, excavation, machinery, cargo, in-game finances, statistics and preferences are stored in the app's local files. The world seed, pile number and starting material quantity are also kept in this device's Keychain to preserve the hidden diamond's identity. These records are used only to save and restore play.

Local diagnostics

When developer performance recording is explicitly enabled, the game writes a local report of frame timing, equipment counts and device thermal state. The app does not upload these reports.

Retention and deletion

Local save files are kept while the app remains installed. Deleting the app using iPhone Settings removes its local files; offloading keeps app data. iOS may retain the Keychain world identity after deletion. Backups are controlled by your device settings. The developer has no server copy of your game data to access or delete.

Your choices

You can change sound, haptics and reduced motion in Settings. There is no advertising or tracking consent to withdraw because the app does not perform those activities. Apple's App Store, TestFlight, backups and device diagnostics are governed by Apple's own privacy settings and policies.

Policy changes

This policy describes the version of Find the diamond installed on this device. If data practices change, the policy and App Store privacy details will be updated.
